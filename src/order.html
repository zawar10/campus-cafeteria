How to Order

Follow these simple steps to enjoy your favorite meals at Campus Bites.

Ordering Process

Getting your food is easy! Just follow these five steps.

01. Browse the Menu

Explore our menu and choose the food or drinks you would like to order.

02. Visit the Cafeteria Counter

Visit the Campus Bites cafeteria counter to place your order in person.

03. Place Your Order and Pay

Tell the staff your selected items and complete your payment at the counter.

04. Receive Your Token

Collect your order token and keep it safe until your food is ready.

05. Collect Your Order

When your order is ready, present your token and collect your food.

Helpful Ordering Guidelines

  • Check the menu before visiting the cafeteria counter.
  • Keep your token safe until you collect your order.
  • Confirm your order before making payment.
  • Follow the cafeteria queue and wait for your turn.
  • Ask cafeteria staff if you need help with your order.
